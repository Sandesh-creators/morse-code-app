Quick Morse Rules

1. DURATION
A dash is exactly 3 times longer than a dot. Perfect for straight key training.
2. INTER-SYMBOL
The space between elements of the same letter is 1 dot length.
3. LETTERS
The gap between letters equals 3 dots (handled by Enter / Auto-Confirm).
4. WORDS
The space between words is 7 dots. Use the Space button!
